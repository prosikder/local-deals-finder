## 3. Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 2026  
**Application:** Local Deals Finder (`com.localdeals.finder`)

### 1. Introduction
Local Deals Finder ("we", "our", or "app") is dedicated to protecting user privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles device information.

### 2. Information Handled
- **Device Location (GPS / Network Location):**
  - The application requests access to `ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION` and `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION` solely to calculate the straight-line Haversine distance between your current coordinates and local merchant venues in Dhaka.
  - **No Off-Device Transmission:** All location calculations are performed **100% locally on your device**. Your location coordinates are **never** transmitted to any external server, cloud backend, analytics provider, or third party.
  - If location permissions are denied, users can manually select a preferred neighborhood from the predefined Dhaka areas list.
- **Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`):**
  - Used strictly for delivering on-device notifications (such as wishlist deal expiration alerts and local order updates). No notification data leaves your device.
- **Local Application Data:**
  - User profiles, wishlists, order histories, and local chat messages are stored exclusively in your local SQLite Room database and encrypted DataStore using Android KeyStore cryptography (AES-GCM).

### 3. Third-Party Services & SDKs
Local Deals Finder does **not** include any third-party advertising SDKs, tracking frameworks, or cloud analytics services (no Google Analytics, no Facebook SDK, no Firebase).

### 4. Children’s Privacy
Our application does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children.

### 5. Contact
For questions regarding this privacy policy, please contact: `support@localdeals.finder` or visit our open-source repository at `https://github.com/prosikder/local-deals-finder`.
